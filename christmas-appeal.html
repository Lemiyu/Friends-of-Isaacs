Questions

Christmas Appeal FAQs

What can I donate?

We're collecting new toys, gifts for teenagers, advent calendars and selection boxes.

Do gifts need to be new?

Yes, please. We can only accept new gifts in their original packaging, so every child gets something brand new to open on Christmas morning.

Should I wrap my gift?

Please leave gifts unwrapped. It means we can check them and match each one to the right child.

What ages are you collecting for?

We're collecting gifts for children and young people aged 0 to 18. Gifts for teenagers are especially welcome, as they're often the hardest to find.

When is the last day to drop off?

The last day for drop-offs is Friday 11 December. See where to drop off your gifts.

Can I donate money instead?

Yes. You can donate via GoFundMe, PayPal or our Amazon Wishlist, or set up a monthly standing order. See all the ways to donate.

Can my business or school run a collection?

We'd love that. Get in touch or call us on 07496 381323 and we'll help you get set up.

My family needs help this Christmas. What should I do?

Please get in touch or call us on 07496 381323. You can also visit any of our food bank sites, open 7 days a week with no referral needed.
